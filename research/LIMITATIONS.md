# Research limitations

- The bundled cases are synthetic software fixtures, not clinical validation.
- No public clinical dataset is bundled or silently downloaded.
- The full pipeline depends on external model and biomedical APIs.
- The primary baseline and full pipeline do not have identical downstream
  evidence calls; this is intentional and documented.
- Literature and case-study outputs are not automatically judged for factual
  support. Qualified human review is required.
- Confidence/ranking scores are not calibrated probabilities.
- Individual component ablations are unavailable until research-only flags are
  added to the orchestrator and verified.
- Two cases are insufficient for statistical inference.
- Results must not be used to claim safety, efficacy or superiority over
  clinical systems.
